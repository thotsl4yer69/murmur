01 — WEAR

Wear the system.
Not the screen.

MURMUR treats the garment as a distributed interface between the person and their environment. The electronics are modular. The wearer remains authoritative. The clothing stays clothing.

WEAR / 00101

Garments as infrastructure.

Electronics are distributed through the garment rather than bolted onto it. Power, sensing, haptics and removable modules are designed as one architecture.

MOLT / 00202
CORESENSORPOWERMODULE

Built to change.

MOLT is the principle of removable hardware. Replace the payload, firmware or garment layer without rebuilding the whole system.
